🃏 Memory Match

Flip two cards at a time. Find all the pairs!

🎨 Made by Reiya & family · 2026

📝 How I made this

Every picture goes in twice, then the cards get shuffled. When you flip two, the game checks if they are the same. If not, it waits a moment so you can remember them, then flips them back.
