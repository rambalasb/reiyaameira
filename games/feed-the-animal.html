🦁 Feed the Animal

Drag the animal to what it eats, or just tap the right box!

🎨 Made by Reiya & family · 2026

📝 How I made this

When you let go of the animal, the game checks which box your finger is over. Every animal in the list knows if it is a herbivore, carnivore or omnivore, and has a fun fact.
