🧭 Unicorn Maze

Help the unicorn get to the rainbow! Use the arrows, your keyboard, or swipe.

🎨 Made by Reiya & family · 2026

📝 How I made this

The maze starts as a grid of boxes with all their walls up. The computer wanders from box to box, knocking down walls as it goes, and walks back when it gets stuck. That makes a new maze every time, with exactly one way through!
