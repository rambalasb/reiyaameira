Day Water Health

Tip: plants need water, sunlight and time. But not too much water!

🎨 Made by Reiya & family · 2026

📝 How I made this

Each night the game checks the plant. If it had some water (but not too much) and sunlight, it grows one step. If not, it loses a heart. The sun dries up the water a little each day.
